This blog is run by the team behind [EntryTarget](https://entrytarget.com/) — dedicated double-entry ledger infrastructure that deploys inside the customer's own AWS account.

## What we write about

- **Engineering notes.** How the Rust runtime handles batching, crash recovery, and back-pressure. Why we chose the boring primitives we chose.
- **Security posture.** Integrity model, key rotation, license signing, where data physically lives, and what we can (and cannot) see from our side of the line.
- **Operations.** The AWS CDK stack we deploy, the IAM template customers review, the heartbeat and license flow that keeps the runtime honest.
- **Accounting primitives.** Why double-entry still wins in 2026, reconciliation patterns, and the operational consequences of asking "where did this number come from?"

We don't publish on a schedule. Posts go out when there's something worth writing down.
